# India-CPI-Inflation-Case-Study
# 📈 CPI Inflation Trend Analysis – India (2017–2024)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes India’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) trends from 2017 to 2024 using Excel-based data analysis and visualization techniques. The objective of the project is to understand inflation patterns across different sectors, evaluate the impact of COVID-19 and global oil prices on inflation, and identify key contributors to CPI growth in India.

The project was completed as part of a Data Analytics learning program to apply analytical thinking, visualization, and economic interpretation to real-world government datasets.

---

## 🔍 Problem Statements

The project aimed to answer the following analytical questions:

- Which broader categories contributed the most to CPI inflation?
- How has CPI inflation changed year-on-year since 2017?
- Which year recorded the highest inflation growth and why?
- How did food inflation behave during the 12 months ending May 2023?
- Which food categories contributed most to inflation changes?
- What was the impact of COVID-19 on India’s inflation trends?
- How did imported crude oil price fluctuations affect CPI inflation between 2021 and 2023?
- Which CPI categories were most sensitive to oil price changes?

---

## 💡 Objectives

- Analyze long-term CPI inflation trends in India
- Understand sector-wise contribution to inflation

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset used in this project is based on India’s Consumer Price Index (CPI) data extracted from Government of India sources.

The CPI basket includes categories such as:
- Food & Beverages
- Housing
- Fuel & Light
- Clothing & Footwear
- Transportation
- Medical Care
- Education
- Recreation & Entertainment

The dataset contains:
- Monthly CPI index values
- Urban and Rural CPI
- General Inflation Index
- Category-wise inflation indicators

## ✅ Key Findings

- Food, Transport, and Housing categories contributed significantly to overall CPI inflation.
- India experienced noticeable inflation spikes during and after the COVID-19 pandemic.
- Food inflation showed high monthly volatility, especially during supply-chain disruptions.
- Healthcare and essential services inflation increased sharply after March 2020.
- Imported crude oil prices strongly influenced transportation and fuel-related inflation categories.
- CPI inflation trends varied significantly across different categories, showing uneven economic impact.
- Global economic disruptions and domestic supply constraints played a major role in inflation movement.

---

## 📌 Recommendations

- Strengthen food price stabilization policies to reduce inflation volatility.
- Monitor transportation and fuel inflation more closely during global oil price fluctuations.
- Develop early warning systems for categories highly sensitive to global economic shocks.
- Improve supply-chain resilience for essential goods during crisis situations.
- Increase policy focus on healthcare inflation management during emergencies.
